The story

Light born from rubble.

In a city crushed by war and forgotten by the world, a barefoot 15-year-old boy named Issa Malak becomes the spark no one saw coming.

A boy who refused to let hate define him.

The Last Prophet of Gaza is an unforgettable tale of light born from rubble, a novel by Cliff St. Hubert.

About the author

Cliff St. Hubert

Cliff St. Hubert is a Miami-based writer, visual artist and television producer at Island TV. He writes historical and speculative fiction about people history tried to overlook, and brings a producer's eye for the cinematic to every page.

He is also the founder of 3rd I AI, a creative studio built for independent storytellers and diaspora creators. His novels include The Man They Called Yasuke, the untold story of the African warrior who became samurai.
